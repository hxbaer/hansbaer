About

From guest to host.

I grew up in Switzerland with Swiss and Asian roots. My surroundings, at school and at home, were always international. Growing up, I stayed in some beautiful hotels, and at some point I became more interested in the other side of the front desk: what it takes to be a good host.

That led me to study hotel management at Les Roches and to start out in hotel front offices in Frankfurt, Berlin, Munich and Gstaad. From there I moved into luxury retail, first at Montblanc in Zürich, then at Louis Vuitton in Stuttgart, and most recently to Diageo, where I looked after premium bars, restaurants and hotels in two territories.

Whether at a front desk, in a boutique or with a bar team, the job has felt much the same to me: look after people properly, and set the standard yourself.

Travel

Away from work

I was given the chance to travel from a young age, and much of it took me to Asia. That was more than twenty years ago, before the world felt as connected as it does now. For a European, Asia was still far away and far less familiar than it seems today.

Those trips shaped how I see things. What stays with me is rarely the sights, but the small things: how a city starts its day, how people host, what ends up on the table. I still travel whenever I can.

Those who think in boundaries will always be defined by them. True growth comes from the connections we build beyond them.
